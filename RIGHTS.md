## What CC0 does and doesn't cover

- **It covers only this project's own work.** Kara Zajac waives her copyright and related rights in the Charter, to the extent the law allows. Rights others hold in material the Charter quotes or draws on are not hers to waive.
- **It doesn't cover names.** CC0 waives copyright, not trademark (CC0 1.0, section 4(a)). The names "Terran Charter", "Terran Humanism" and "Terran Commonwealth" sit outside the dedication.
- **A request, not a condition.** If you adapt the Charter, please say so and link to the original, so readers can find the current version and its history. Nothing in CC0 requires this.
