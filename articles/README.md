# The Articles of Terra

*The second layer of [The Terran Charter](../CHARTER.md) · third working draft, 12 October 2026*

The Articles set out how the Commonwealth's institutions work. The Foundations prevail over them, and they prevail over the Schedules, which hold the numbers they use (Part V.3).

| Article | What it covers | From the second draft |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [1. The Culture of Enough](1-the-culture-of-enough.md) | Greed and enough, the wealth limit, taxes, the commons, and business | Parts IV and V |
| [2. Working Together](2-working-together.md) | Digital citizenship, what is planetary, open logs, the doughnut test, separate powers, and the Council of Communities | Part VII; VIII.8, VIII.10, VIII.13 |
| [3. How Terrans Decide](3-how-terrans-decide.md) | Proposals, assemblies and panels drawn by lot, the vote of all terrans, the ceiling, and decisions as versions | VIII.1–4, VIII.11, VIII.12, VIII.22, VIII.24 |
| [4. Votes, Draws and Offices](4-votes-draws-and-offices.md) | Secure votes, public draws, certified candidates, and the independent offices | VIII.5, VIII.6, VIII.16–18 |
| [5. The Stewards](5-the-stewards.md) | The Council of Stewards, the Oversight Council, and rules made under an act | VIII.7, VIII.14, VIII.15 |
| [6. Emergencies](6-emergencies.md) | Emergencies that expire, their limits, continuity, and urgent acts | VIII.19–21, VIII.23 |

**Reading them.** A clause is cited by Article and number, so 3.4 is Article 3, clause 4. Parts I–V are the Parts of the Foundations, and the Principles are the Seven Principles in Part II. Each number in the text links to the entry in the Schedules that sets it.

**Changing them.** An Article changes only by two votes of all terrans, held at least a year apart, each with a majority of the votes overall and in a majority of the world regions (Part V.5).
