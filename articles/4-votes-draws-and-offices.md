# Article 4 — Votes, Draws and Offices

*One of the nine [Articles of Terra](README.md) in [The Terran Charter](../CHARTER.md) · [Commentary](../commentary/articles.md#article-4--votes-draws-and-offices)*

Every terran's vote is secret and checked, every draw by lot is public, and no single body holds the keys.

1. **Secure votes.** A terran's digital identity proves they may vote but never reveals how they voted. Terrans may vote online or in person. An in-person vote is final and replaces any online vote, whenever cast. Among online votes, the last counts. Online voting closes before in-person voting does, so a vote made online under pressure can be replaced until voting closes.
2. **Every vote is checked.** Every result is audited before it takes effect. Where an audit fails, the paper record is counted by hand. A new vote is held only if there is no paper record, or it is lost, and the missing votes could change the result. Votes that change this Charter are also cast on paper, so they never depend on software alone. Voting keys and systems are held by [five][key-holders] independent bodies, any [three][key-quorum] of which can act together, so no single failure can lose a vote.
3. **Drawn fairly.** Every draw by lot uses a public random seed, is run in parallel by at least [three][draw-operators] independent operators, and can be reproduced by anyone. No body oversees the draws for its own seats. Draws from all terrans are stratified by region, age, gender, education, and language. The identity register behind them is kept by at least [two][register-custodians] independent custodians, audited yearly, with a paper fallback. Service by lot is voluntary, paid, and protected: no one may lose their work or their home for serving, and members of a deciding panel are named only after their vote. Wherever this Charter counts regions, it means the world regions in Schedule 3.
4. **Certified candidates.** Candidates for Steward, judge, ombudsman, and the independent offices are certified against criteria published in advance by planetary act. Anyone who meets the criteria may enrol on an open roster. Each certifying panel is drawn for one round, with a majority drawn by lot from all terrans and experts drawn from the rosters. Panels publish their reasons, no one sits on two, and a refusal can be appealed to the Planetary Court.
5. **Independent offices.** The Audit Office, the Statistics Office, the Identity and Voting Office, and the Earth Science Panel are independent. Their members are drawn by lot from certified candidates, and the Earth Science Panel adds lay reviewers drawn by lot. Members serve fixed terms of [eight years][office-term], may be removed only for proven misconduct, and take no orders from the Stewards. Each office's budget is a fixed share of planetary revenue.

*Public draws follow the IETF's verifiable lotteries.*
