# Article 5 — The Stewards

*One of the nine [Articles of Terra](README.md) in [The Terran Charter](../CHARTER.md) · [Commentary](../commentary/articles.md#article-5--the-stewards)*

Stewards carry out what terrans decide, and two councils choose them, watch them, and can remove them.

1. **Stewards carry out decisions.** A Council of [seven][stewards] Stewards carries out planetary acts, holds no veto over them, and commands no army. No more than [two][stewards-per-region] come from one region. Each heads a department and serves one [six-year][steward-term] term, counting any part of a term; two or three seats are filled every [two years][steward-round]. Stewards are chosen from a certified pool (4.4) after public hearings, with the approval of both the Oversight Council (5.2) and the Council of Communities (2.10), and either council may remove one by [two-thirds][steward-removal]. A Steward whose term ends stays until a successor is approved. A seat still unfilled [six months][steward-fallback] after a term ends or falls vacant is filled by lot from candidates either council approved. The Stewards lead a civil service hired by open competition and protected from political dismissal.
2. **The Oversight Council.** The Oversight Council has [150][oversight-size] terrans drawn by lot, serving staggered [two-year][oversight-term] terms. It holds public hearings with candidates for Steward, approves or removes Stewards with the Council of Communities, and reviews the Stewards' work in public. Its members may not become Stewards within [five years][oversight-cooling], and the Planetary Court may remove a member for misconduct.
3. **Rules under an act.** The Stewards and the civil service may make detailed rules only under a planetary act that says what is routine. Each draft is published, and only comments from verified or sampled terrans count. A panel of [200][rule-panel] terrans drawn by lot reviews it for the assembly whose power it falls under. The rule takes effect after an objection window of [60 days][objection-window] unless that assembly calls it in. A call-in can be forced by [100,000][call-in-signatures] terrans of voting age or [a tenth][call-in-council] of the Council of Communities.

*The Council of Stewards draws on Switzerland's Federal Council.*
