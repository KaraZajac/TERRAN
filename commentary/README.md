# The Commentary

*A commentary on [The Terran Charter](../CHARTER.md) · not binding*

The Commentary explains why each part of the Charter says what it says, and where the evidence is thin. It binds no one. Where it and the Charter differ, the Charter governs, and where it and Kara's intent differ, hers does.
