# Schedule 1 — Safeguards

*Generated from [numbers.yaml](numbers.yaml) by `make schedules`: edit the YAML, not this file · [All Schedules](README.md) · [The Terran Charter](../CHARTER.md)*

These numbers hold power in check. They cap wealth and pay, limit terms and emergencies, protect terrans' power to propose and to call a vote, and fix the size of the bodies that guard against capture. They change only as the Articles do (Part V.6), so a passing majority can't use an ordinary act to loosen a check on itself. Only the values bind. Each range shows where the research suggests the number could sensibly sit, as advice for any future vote, and each reason explains the choice.
