# Schedule 5 — Hazards

*[All Schedules](README.md) · [The Terran Charter](../CHARTER.md) · hazards are added or removed by planetary act, though not by an urgent act; what is never a hazard changes only as the Articles do (Part V.6)*

The Council of Stewards may declare an emergency only for a hazard on this list (6.1). Like every planetary act, the list stays within the planetary powers (2.4), so hazards that communities can meet on their own are theirs to meet.

1. A pandemic, or another disease spreading fast across regions.
2. The use, threatened use, theft or loss of a weapon of mass destruction, or of the material to make one.
3. A crisis in the Commonwealth's own money, once it has one.

These are never hazards: peaceful protest or dissent; a vote, a draw, or their result; and a slow breach of the ceiling, which ceiling acts address.
