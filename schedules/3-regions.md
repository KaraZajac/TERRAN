# Schedule 3 — World Regions

*[All Schedules](README.md) · [The Terran Charter](../CHARTER.md) · changes only as the Foundations do (Part V.6)*

Not yet chosen. Wherever this Charter counts regions, it means at least ten world regions, starting from an existing statistical classification. Each terran counts in the region where they live.

For reference, regions count in six places:

- petitions to an assembly, and calls for a vote of all terrans, must be spread across the regions (3.1, 3.5)
- the Council of Communities' seats are shared among the regions (2.10)
- ceiling acts need majorities of delegates from both lower-footprint and higher-footprint regions (3.6)
- each region is limited in how many Stewards it may have (5.1)
- every draw from all terrans is stratified by region (4.3)
- a change to the Articles needs majorities in a majority of the regions (Part V.5)
