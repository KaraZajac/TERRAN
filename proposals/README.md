# Proposals

Worked proposals for the next draft. Each one turns research into a concrete design: what would change, the evidence for it, what it costs, what's untested, and candidate wording in the Charter's voice. Nothing here changes the Charter until Kara adopts it. When she does, the change goes into the [Charter](../CHARTER.md), its [Articles](../articles/README.md) or its [Schedules](../schedules/README.md), with a line in [CHANGELOG.md](../CHANGELOG.md).

Status: isonomy.md was written on 12 October 2026 at Kara's request and awaits her decision. modern-terran-government.md was adopted into the second working draft on 12 October 2026. The third working draft, the same day, moved its clauses into the Articles of Terra; the [changelog](../CHANGELOG.md#third-working-draft--12-october-2026) maps the numbers.

| Proposal | Parts | What it proposes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [isonomy.md](isonomy.md) | Articles 2–4, 7, 8; Schedule 1 | Name the form of government an isonomy, after Otanes' three marks: lot, account, and deliberation in public. Draw two lots: a common lot from all terrans that decides, and a qualified lot from open, earnable, lapsing pools that drafts, reviews and certifies. Add Athens' checks before and after office. |
| [modern-terran-government.md](modern-terran-government.md) | IV, VII–X, XII | Replace universal votes on every question with samples of terrans drawn by lot that draft and decide, a vote of all terrans on demand, a council of community delegates holding a second key, a short list of planetary powers, a small collegial executive, emergencies that expire, and decisions treated as versions. It phases in, earning binding power stage by stage. |
