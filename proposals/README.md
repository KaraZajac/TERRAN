# Proposals

Worked proposals for the next draft. Each one turns research into a concrete design: what would change, the evidence for it, what it costs, what's untested, and candidate wording in the Charter's voice. Nothing here changes the Charter until Kara adopts it. When she does, the change goes into the [Charter](../CHARTER.md), its [Articles](../articles/README.md) or its [Schedules](../schedules/README.md), with a line in [CHANGELOG.md](../CHANGELOG.md).

Status: modern-terran-government.md was adopted into the second working draft on 12 October 2026. The third working draft, the same day, moved its clauses into the Articles of Terra; the [changelog](../CHANGELOG.md#third-working-draft--12-october-2026) maps the numbers.

It phases in, earning binding power stage by stage. |
