# Proposals

Worked proposals for the next draft. Each one turns research into a concrete design: what would change, the evidence for it, what it costs, what's untested, and candidate wording in the Charter's voice. Nothing here changes the Charter until Kara adopts it. When she does, the change goes into [CHARTER.md](../CHARTER.md) with a line in [CHANGELOG.md](../CHANGELOG.md).

| Proposal | Parts | What it proposes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [modern-terran-government.md](modern-terran-government.md) | IV, VII–X, XII | Replace universal votes on every question with samples of terrans drawn by lot that draft and decide, a vote of all terrans on demand, a council of community delegates holding a second key, a short list of planetary powers, a small collegial executive, emergencies that expire, and decisions treated as versions. It phases in, earning binding power stage by stage. |
