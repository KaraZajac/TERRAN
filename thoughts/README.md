# Thoughts

The reasoning behind the Charter. The files here cite the twelve Parts of the first and second drafts; the [changelog](../CHANGELOG.md#third-working-draft--12-october-2026) maps them to the third draft's Foundations and Articles. For a reader's guide to the current text, part by part, see the [Commentary](../commentary/README.md).

| File | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| [research-to-charter.md](research-to-charter.md) | How the drafting session's research (9 October 2026) became the draft (10 October 2026): 37 recommendations traced to clauses, the objections the draft answers, and what it left out. |
| [design-notes.md](design-notes.md) | How the draft is built: the nine design moves that recur across its twelve Parts, and where they pull against each other. |
| [pressure-points.md](pressure-points.md) | Where the research notes say the draft is under strain, across all Parts. |

The drafting conversation itself wasn't kept, only its two documents, and both are in this repository. The files here are built from those documents, the research notes and a close reading of the text, and each says who wrote it. Kara's own notes on why the Charter says what it says belong here too, and take precedence over any reading of the text.
